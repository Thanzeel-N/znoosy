Current update: the large comfort-guide homepage section and its navigation link have been removed. Its seven explanations now appear as a compact “Mattress types & feel” list under Support, also accessible through “Understand materials & feel” in product details. The list has no images or animations. The earlier photographic treatment described below is retained as design history only; its images remain saved locally for potential reuse.

## Reference and changes

Reviewed all eight slides of the [Queens Sleep carousel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbV-VztjTR_/): the title slide and seven mattress descriptions. The useful idea is to explain what shoppers are choosing before asking them to choose a product.

znoosy now includes a static photographic comfort guide, construction labels on the collection, separate construction/feel fields in product details, and an inline FAQ. The guide uses znoosy's existing warm neutral palette and typography with three original generated material photographs. No competitor photos, logos, or slide designs are embedded.

## The seven comfort-guide topics

| Topic | What the guide explains | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Memory foam | A contouring comfort material that may be used in foam or hybrid builds | Helps shoppers understand a cradled sensation without confusing the material with a firmness rating. |
| Innerspring | A support core built with springs and cushioning above it | Explains where a responsive, springy feel can come from. |
| Hybrid | Comfort layers combined with a spring core | Shows how cushioning and spring response can coexist. |
| Orthopedic | A support-focused product description | Encourages questions about actual specifications instead of assuming a health benefit from a label. |
| Pillow top | An additional upholstered surface cushion | Separates a soft first impression from the firmness of the mattress underneath. |
| Firm | A more resistant surface feel | Explains preference without presenting firmness as a quality ranking. |
| Soft | A more yielding surface feel | Explains plushness while reminding shoppers to examine the support underneath. |

These are not seven mutually exclusive constructions. A mattress may be a hybrid, have a pillow top, and still be described as firm. The guide makes this distinction explicit.

## Interactions

- The guide has no animated tabs, diagrams, sliders, image zoom, or scroll reveal. All seven topics are visible in the page flow; optional reading notes use native disclosure controls without animation.
- Original generated photographs show foam, fabric-wrapped springs, and a quilted hybrid mattress. The local WebP assets are illustrative, not manufacturing specifications. Asset paths and exact prompts are recorded in `COMFORT-PHOTOGRAPHY.md`.
- The guide connects to the existing mattress finder. Product details provide a link back to the guide.
- Search includes construction terms such as “hybrid”.
- Buyer FAQs use native keyboard-accessible disclosure controls.
- Layout adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile; existing reduced-motion preferences remain respected.
